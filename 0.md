Topic 0

A. What is COSE?
COSE simply stands for Common Sense.
It is a way to approach any domain of knowledge through the lens of first-principles thinking and reasoning.
Before any advanced or specialized knowledge can be understood, there must be a basic foundation that underlies that knowledge.

Hence, advanced knowledge can be understood as developments of that basic reasoning applied to even more complex and systemic objects, subjects, systems, and their environments.

B. What is OLS?
OLS, or Open Learning System, is the learning framework used to provide the knowledge in the curriculum.
An open learning system emphasizes the understanding of knowledge in connected areas rather than treating every subject as an isolated topic.
A concept introduced in one subject can therefore provide a foundation for concepts introduced later.
It provides a systematic way of approaching different subjects while allowing the relationships between them to remain visible.

OLS consequently provides the structure within which COSE and the major are brought together.
COSE provides a foundation for reasoning, while OLS provides a framework for organizing that reasoning into a structured body of knowledge.

C. COSE OLS and its relationship with the major
COSE OLS refers to the combination of Common Sense and the Open Learning System within the curriculum.
COSE provides the basic reasoning foundation, while OLS provides the system for organizing, connecting, and developing knowledge.

Together, they establish the general learning environment in which the major is studied.
Rather than beginning immediately with specialized management concepts, the curriculum first establishes a common foundation for understanding objects, resources, organizations, information, measurement, and environmental behavior.

This relationship can be represented conceptually as:

COSE → reasoning foundation
OLS → learning and knowledge structure
COSE–OLS → integrated learning framework
Major → specialized application to resource-managing organizations

Through this structure, the learner can progress from basic understanding toward increasingly specialized knowledge without losing sight of the relationships between the different subjects.

D. What is Management of Resource-Managing Organizations?

In human terms, it is the study of Business Management.

E. What is the purpose of building COSE?

To make the contents of the undergraduate Business Management major easier to understand and aligned with the author’s preferences.
